Construction & Materials

Built from the panel up to survive the trip.

Every SharkCrate starts with 19/32″ (15mm) oriented strand board — dense, void-free, and stable under load. We wrap every panel edge in a 0.040″ (1.0mm) steel bracket, a detail unique to SharkCrates that does two jobs at once: it shields the corners and seams from impact damage, and it locks the whole assembly into a far more rigid structure than screws or staples alone.

  • ✓Panel material: 19/32″ (15mm) OSB, rated for repeated reuse.
  • ✓Edge protection: 0.040″ (1.0mm) formed steel brackets on every panel.
  • ✓Assembly hardware: pallet strapping, ratchet straps, or screws — included with every crate.
  • ✓Reusability: panels nest and re-nest without losing structural integrity.

A fully collapsed SharkCrate stands about 10″ tall — roughly 75% less space than an assembled crate.

Collapsible Design

Assemble in minutes. Collapse just as fast.

The panel system that makes SharkCrates strong is also what makes them fast to build: each crate ships with its panels individually banded, and most customers nest them into a finished crate in 3–5 minutes with no special tools.

When the shipment's done, the same crate folds back down to about 10 inches tall. That means you can stack collapsed crates up to eight high on a single pallet — cutting inbound freight costs dramatically. In one customer case study, switching to flat-shipped SharkCrates cut freight spend by $339.08, a 75% reduction, on a single lane.

Certification

ISPM-15 certified, so your shipment doesn't stall at the border.

International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 governs wood packaging used in international trade. Every SharkCrate is manufactured and heat-treated to meet ISPM-15, and stamped accordingly — so you can export to virtually any country without extra fumigation, delays, or rejected shipments at customs.

Globally recognized

Accepted by customs authorities in nearly every country that trades internationally.

Stamped & documented

Every crate carries the official IPPC mark so paperwork checks are a formality, not a bottleneck.

Zero extra steps for you

Treatment is built into manufacturing — there's nothing additional to schedule or pay for before you ship.
